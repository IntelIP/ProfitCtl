# Design-Partner Targeting Rubric

Use this rubric to decide whether a company is worth adding to the first 10 design-partner targets.

## Target Profile

Prioritize teams that already have a pricing, packaging, or contract decision in motion.

Best-fit signals:

- B2B SaaS or software-led services
- recurring revenue matters to the business
- pricing changes are active or upcoming
- a founder, product lead, finance lead, RevOps lead, or CS lead owns the question
- they can share real inputs instead of generic hypotheticals

## Scoring

Score each target from `0` to `2` in every category.

- `2`: strong fit
- `1`: partial fit
- `0`: weak or unknown fit

Total possible score: `16`

### 1. Decision Urgency

- `2`: pricing, packaging, or contract decision is active now
- `1`: decision is likely this quarter
- `0`: no decision in motion

### 2. Revenue Shape

- `2`: recurring revenue or recurring contract economics are central
- `1`: mixed recurring and one-time revenue
- `0`: mostly one-time or unrelated to recurring economics

### 3. Input Quality

- `2`: can share real numbers, exports, or config shapes
- `1`: can share partial inputs
- `0`: cannot share useful inputs

### 4. Install Readiness

- `2`: comfortable with CLI or Bun package install
- `1`: willing to try with help
- `0`: wants a hosted-only workflow now

### 5. Feedback Value

- `2`: likely to tell us whether the output changed a decision
- `1`: likely to evaluate but not commit to follow-up
- `0`: unlikely to provide useful feedback

### 6. Expansion Potential

- `2`: multiple people will review or rely on the output
- `1`: one person owns the decision
- `0`: no clear follow-on use

### 7. Benchmark Match

- `2`: one of the existing benchmark pairs maps cleanly
- `1`: close but needs translation
- `0`: requires a custom one-off model first

### 8. Outreach Practicality

- `2`: easy to contact and follow up
- `1`: possible with extra effort
- `0`: too hard to reach or nurture

## Qualification Thresholds

- `12+`: target immediately
- `8-11`: keep as backup target
- `< 8`: do not spend design-partner time yet

## Disqualifiers

Do not target if they:

- only want generic finance planning
- want a hosted collaboration product immediately
- cannot install the CLI or run Bun
- cannot articulate a pricing or contract decision
- are asking for bespoke consulting work instead of product evaluation

## What To Record

For every target, capture:

- company name
- contact name and role
- target score
- pricing or contract question
- benchmark pair to lead with
- outreach status
- last touch date
- follow-up next step
