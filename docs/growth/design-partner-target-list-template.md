# First 10 Design-Partner Targets

Use this as the working list for the first evaluator pipeline.

## List Rules

- keep exactly 10 active targets at a time
- update weekly
- replace weak targets fast
- do not add targets without a clear question and a benchmark match

## Suggested Fields

- `Target Score`: from [Design-Partner Targeting Rubric](design-partner-targeting-rubric.md)
- `Decision Question`: the one pricing or contract question they need answered
- `Benchmark Pair`: one of the current benchmark pairs or a close equivalent
- `Outreach Status`: `not contacted`, `contacted`, `replied`, `installed`, `activated`, `calibrated`, `design partner`
- `Next Step`: the next concrete action, not a vague note

## Weekly Operating Rule

If a target has no response after three touches and no clear reason to keep it, replace it with a better fit.
